GdA Companion — get it on your phone

One minute, three steps, no accounts, nothing to pay. Once it's done you'll have an app icon on your phone that works even with no signal.

1

Point your camera at this square

Open the phone's Camera like you're taking a photo. Hold it over the square below. A little button appears — tap it.

2

Add it to your home screen

iPhone: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, bottom of the screen), scroll down, tap “Add to Home Screen”, then Add.

Android: tap the three dots (top right), then “Add to Home screen”, then Add.

3

That's it — look for the gold GdA icon

From now on, just tap the icon like any other app. It works at the club even if the Wi-Fi is down.

If the camera trick doesn't work: open the phone's internet browser (Safari or Chrome), type the address printed under the square, and carry on from step 2. Or hand the phone to Andy.
